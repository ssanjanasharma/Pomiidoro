Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got cute answers~ 💬

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo. You work for 25 minutes (one "pomodoro"), then take a 5-minute short break. After 4 pomodoros, you take a longer 15-30 minute break. It helps maintain focus and prevent burnout! ✨

Absolutely! Head to the Customize section on the home page. You can adjust the Pomodoro time (default 25 min), Short Break (default 5 min), and Long Break (default 15 min) to whatever works best for you~ 🎯

In the Customize panel on the home page, scroll down to "Pick a Theme". You can choose from Tomato 🍅, Froggie 🐸, Bunny 🐰, Bear 🐻, Melon 🍈, or Valentine 💖. Each theme changes the whole color palette of the app!
